<section id="inAppInbox" class="inapp-popover hidden" aria-label="مركز الإشعارات"><div class="inapp-popover-head"><h2>الإشعارات</h2><button id="markAllRead" type="button">قراءة الكل</button></div><div id="inAppList" class="inapp-list"><div class="empty">جارٍ تحميل الإشعارات...</div></div><button id="refreshInApp" type="button" style="margin-top:10px;background:#27496b">تحديث</button></section>

4) In renderInAppNotifications(), replace the old badge update:
badge.innerHTML=unread?`<span class="inapp-badge">${unread}</span>`:'';
with:
badge.textContent=unread>99?"99+":String(unread);badge.classList.toggle("hidden",unread===0);

5) Immediately after:
$('refreshInApp').addEventListener('click',loadNotifications);
add:
$('inAppBell').addEventListener('click',()=>{const panel=$('inAppInbox'),open=panel.classList.contains('hidden');panel.classList.toggle('hidden',!open);$('inAppBell').setAttribute('aria-expanded',String(open));if(open)loadNotifications()});
document.addEventListener('click',e=>{const panel=$('inAppInbox');if(!panel.classList.contains('hidden')&&!panel.contains(e.target)&&!$('inAppBell').contains(e.target)){panel.classList.add('hidden');$('inAppBell').setAttribute('aria-expanded','false')}});
